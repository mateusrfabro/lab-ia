How Soft-Pull Credit Card Pre-Qualification Works

Major US card issuers allow consumers to check their approval odds and estimated credit limits using a soft credit inquiry. Unlike a hard inquiry, a soft pull is only visible to you on your credit report and never lowers your FICO score.

Before submitting a formal application, review the three core approval factors: your current Credit Utilization Ratio (ideally below 10% to 30%), on-time payment history over the last 12 months, and total household income.
