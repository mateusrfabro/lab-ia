The 6 Best Personal Budgeting & Expense Tracker Apps in the US (2026 Comparison)

L
Lucas Silveira, CFP® Candidate
October 11, 2026 7 min read
Share:

Since the retirement of Mint, US consumers have migrated to modern budgeting and net-worth trackers that sync securely via Plaid and Finicity. We evaluated the top six platforms based on privacy, bank connectivity, and pricing.

1. Empower Personal Dashboard: Best 100% Free Net Worth & Investment Tracker

Formerly Personal Capital, Empower's free dashboard links your checking, credit cards, 401(k), IRA, and mortgage accounts in one view, offering a powerful Retirement Fee Analyzer at zero subscription cost.

2. YNAB (You Need A Budget): Best for Zero-Based Envelope Budgeting

Built around the rule 'Give Every Dollar a Job,' YNAB forces you to budget only money currently sitting in your bank account rather than forecasting future paychecks—helping new users save an average of $600 in their first two months.

"Choose a zero-based envelope app if you want to stop overspending, or a free net-worth dashboard if your goal is tracking investments and retirement fees."

3. Monarch Money: Best Modern All-in-One Mint Alternative

Designed for couples and households, Monarch combines clean cash-flow rules, recurring subscription detection, and collaborative partner views with zero third-party ad clutter.

4. Fidelity Full View & Credit Karma: Free Built-In Alternatives

If you already hold a Fidelity brokerage or retirement account, Full View provides institutional-grade spending categorization completely free without sharing data with ad networks.
