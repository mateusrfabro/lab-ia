7 Free Productivity & Security Tools Every Remote Worker Should Use in 2026

L
Lucas Silveira, CFP® Candidate
October 27, 2026 7 min read
Share:

Working from home requires managing tax receipts, client contracts, and dozens of logins without an enterprise IT department behind you. These seven free tools keep your workflow fast and secure.

1. Bitwarden: Audited Open-Source Password Manager

Reusing the same password across sites is the #1 cause of account takeovers. Bitwarden's free tier stores unlimited encrypted logins across unlimited devices and generates unique 20-character passwords automatically.

2. Adobe Scan & Apple Notes Scanner: Clean PDF Document Digitization

Avoid sketchy free scanner apps that inject watermarks or upload sensitive tax documents to unknown servers. Native iOS Notes scanning and Adobe Scan create crisp, searchable PDFs for free.

"An open-source password manager combined with hardware or app-based 2FA eliminates 99% of credential-stuffing attacks."

3. Notion & Obsidian: Personal Knowledge and Tax Receipt Tracking

Maintain a simple Notion database for tracking 1099 client invoices, deductible software receipts, and project deadlines in one searchable workspace.

4. 2FAS or Ente Auth: Encrypted Two-Factor Authentication With Cloud Backup

Unlike basic authenticator apps that lock you out if your phone breaks, open-source 2FA apps with end-to-end encrypted backups ensure you never lose access to your financial accounts.
