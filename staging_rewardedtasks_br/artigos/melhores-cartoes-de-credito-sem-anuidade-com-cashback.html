Best No-Annual-Fee Cash-Back Credit Cards in the US: How to Maximize Everyday Rewards

C
Carlos Prado, Cybersecurity Editor
September 27, 2026 8 min read
Share:

For households that pay their statement balance in full every month, a no-annual-fee cash-back credit card provides an automatic 2% to 5% discount on everyday purchases along with strong federal fraud protections under the Fair Credit Billing Act.

Flat-Rate 2% Cash Back vs. 5% Category Bonus Cards

A flat-rate 2% card (earning 1% when you buy and 1% when you pay) serves as the ideal foundation for uncategorized spending, while a no-annual-fee 3% to 5% card targets groceries, gas stations, and dining.

Credit Score Tiers Required for Approval

Most prime cash-back cards require a Good to Excellent FICO score (typically 670 to 740+). Checking your pre-qualification status via soft-pull tools on issuer websites allows you to gauge approval odds without impacting your credit score.

"Credit card rewards only build wealth when your statement balance hits $0.00 before the grace period expires every single month."

Secured Cash-Back Cards for Building or Rebuilding Credit

If your FICO score is below 620 or you are establishing US credit history for the first time, secured cards backed by a refundable $200 security deposit report monthly to all three credit bureaus and often graduate to unsecured status within 6 to 12 months.

The Golden Rule: Never Carry a Balance for Rewards

Earning 2% cash back while paying 22% APR on a revolving balance is a losing math equation. Set up automatic payment for the full statement balance every month to keep rewards 100% tax-free and interest-free.
