Website & App Usability Testing: How to Earn $10 per 15-Minute Test on Verified UX Platforms

Unlike penny surveys that pay $0.50 for 20 minutes, UX (User Experience) testing platforms pay $10 for a standard 15-to-20-minute screen-recorded session—and $30 to $60 for live 30-minute interviews with product designers.

How UX Usability Testing Works

Software companies upload unreleased website checkouts or mobile app prototypes and pay everyday consumers to speak their thoughts out loud while completing specific tasks (such as 'Find a flight to Chicago under $300').

How to Pass the Initial 5-Star Practice Test

Reviewers grade your sample test on two criteria: 1) Clear, quiet audio from a dedicated microphone; and 2) Continuous verbal narration explaining *why* you are clicking or hesitating on each button.

"Speaking your thought process clearly out loud is the difference between a 5-star tester who receives daily invites and an account that sits empty."

Top 4 Legitimate UX Testing Portals Paying via PayPal

UserTesting, Userlytics, Intellizoom, and Trymata pay reliably via verified PayPal transfers 7 to 14 days after session approval.

Maximizing Live Moderated Conversation Invites

Fill out your employment industry, software tools used at work, and device list completely—B2B software researchers frequently pay $60 for 45-minute live Zoom feedback sessions.
