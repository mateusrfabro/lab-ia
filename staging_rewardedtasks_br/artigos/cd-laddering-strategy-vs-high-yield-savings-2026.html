CD Laddering Strategy in 2026: How to Lock In Fixed Bank Yields Without Losing Liquidity

M
Mariana Castro, Financial Analyst
November 06, 2026 8 min read
Share:

While High-Yield Savings Accounts (HYSAs) can lower their variable APY overnight whenever the Federal Reserve adjusts benchmark rates, an FDIC-insured Certificate of Deposit (CD) locks in a guaranteed fixed yield for the entire term.

How a 4-Rung CD Ladder Solves the Liquidity Problem

Instead of locking $20,000 into a single 12-month CD, divide the funds into four $5,000 CDs maturing in 3, 6, 9, and 12 months. Every 90 days, one rung matures—giving you penalty-free cash access or the option to roll it into a new 12-month CD.

No-Penalty CDs vs. Traditional Fixed CDs

Several online FDIC-insured banks offer 11-month or 12-month No-Penalty CDs that allow you to withdraw your full principal and accrued interest starting 7 days after funding with zero early withdrawal fee.

"A 4-rung CD ladder gives you fixed-rate protection against Fed rate cuts while unlocking 25% of your cash every 90 days."

Brokered CDs vs. Direct Bank CDs

Purchasing brokered CDs inside a Fidelity, Schwab, or Vanguard account lets you compare dozens of FDIC-insured banks in one screen without opening separate checking logins.

Marking Maturity Grace Periods on Your Calendar

Most bank CDs provide a 7-to-10-day maturity grace period before auto-renewing into a new term. Set a calendar reminder 10 days prior so you always control your rollover.
