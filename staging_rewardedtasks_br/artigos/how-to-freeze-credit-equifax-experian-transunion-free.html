Credit Freeze vs. Credit Lock: How to Freeze Your Equifax, Experian & TransUnion Files for Free

C
Carlos Prado, Cybersecurity Editor
November 06, 2026 7 min read
Share:

After major corporate and healthcare data breaches exposed hundreds of millions of Social Security Numbers, keeping your credit reports permanently frozen until the exact day you apply for a loan is essential hygiene for every American adult.

Federal Law Guarantees 100% Free Credit Freezes (Economic Growth, Regulatory Relief, and Consumer Protection Act)

Since September 2018, federal law requires Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion to let consumers freeze and unfreeze (thaw) their credit files online or by phone completely free of charge.

Credit Freeze vs. Commercial 'Credit Lock': Do Not Pay for Upsells

Credit bureaus heavily market paid 'Credit Lock' memberships costing $19.99+/month. A statutory Security Freeze is 100% free and carries stronger federal legal protections under the FCRA than a contractual lock.

"Never pay a monthly subscription fee to lock your credit—a federal Security Freeze is 100% free and legally stronger."

How Fast Does a Temporary Thaw Take?

When you need to apply for a mortgage, auto loan, or credit card, logging into your bureau accounts to schedule a temporary 24-hour thaw takes less than 3 minutes and activates almost instantaneously.

Don't Forget Innovis and NCTUE

For comprehensive protection, also place a free security freeze with Innovis (the fourth consumer credit bureau) and the National Consumer Telecom & Utilities Exchange (NCTUE).
