How to Become a Remote Virtual Assistant in the US: Services, Hourly Rates & Client Acquisition

M
Mariana Castro, Financial Analyst
October 13, 2026 8 min read
Share:

Small business owners, real estate brokers, and independent consultants spend up to 15 hours a week on inbox triage, calendar scheduling, and client invoicing. Offering dedicated remote Virtual Assistant (VA) retainers provides recurring monthly income from home.

High-Value VA Services That Command $25 to $45+ Per Hour

General data entry faces heavy competition, but specialized US virtual assistants who handle executive calendar management, CRM follow-ups (HubSpot/Salesforce), podcast/social scheduling, or basic QuickBooks Online reconciliation earn $25 to $50/hour.

Structuring Monthly Retainer Packages Instead of Hourly Billing

Pitching a 20-hour monthly retainer at $650/month gives business owners predictable expenses while guaranteeing your baseline income across 3 to 4 recurring clients.

"Get a free Employer Identification Number (EIN) in 5 minutes on IRS.gov so you never have to put your personal Social Security Number on client W-9 forms."

Essential Software Stack for Remote Operations

Master Google Workspace, Slack, Notion/Asana, Loom for asynchronous video updates, and LastPass or 1Password for receiving client access permissions without ever seeing raw passwords in plain text.

Independent Contractor Agreements and W-9 Compliance

Always sign a clear Statement of Work (SOW) defining response-time windows, confidentiality, and invoice terms, and provide clients with a completed IRS Form W-9 (using a free EIN from IRS.gov rather than your personal SSN).
