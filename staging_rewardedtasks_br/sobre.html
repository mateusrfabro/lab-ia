About RewardedTasks US & Our Editorial Standards

RewardedTasks US (rewardedtasks.com) is an independent consumer finance and digital productivity publication dedicated to helping American households make informed financial decisions, evaluate legitimate side-hustle platforms, and protect their personal data online.

Our Testing & Fact-Checking Methodology

  • Primary Federal Sources: Every guide covering credit scores, banking regulations, tax credits, or consumer fraud references primary US government publications, including the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB), Internal Revenue Service (IRS), Federal Trade Commission (FTC), and Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC).
  • Hands-On App Audits: Before listing any cash-back portal, survey panel, or budgeting app, our editorial team tests the registration flow, minimum payout thresholds, and privacy terms.
  • Zero Get-Rich-Quick Claims: We strictly prohibit exaggerated income claims. Micro-task apps and cash-back programs are presented transparently as supplementary budget tools.
