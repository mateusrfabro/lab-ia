Our Framework

How Audience Assessment Works

Our three-stage evaluation cycle ensures brands receive uncompromised feedback while compensating authenticated participants.

01

Targeted Content Sampling

Sponsored media and creative variants are ingested into short-form test feeds. Every asset is tagged by creative angle, product category, and target audience demographic.

02

Objective Usability Feedback

Participants watch the creative material in controlled conditions and submit qualitative assessments on relevance, clarity, audio-visual quality, and message retention.

03

Audited Payouts & Analytics

Upon passing biometric and device verification checks, reviewers unlock their agreed compensation. Publishers receive aggregated intelligence reports in compliance with privacy regulations.
